About us

Our story

A little alchemy in every pack

We started with one question: why should a great burger sauce take a dozen ingredients and an hour of prep? Our answer was a premix. Roasted chillies, garlic, smoked spices and herbs, measured and blended so you only need to add mayonnaise or water.

Today we make seven flavours, from Indian classics like tandoori mayo to American favourites like Nashville hot and buffalo. Each one is tested batch after batch so it tastes the same every time you open a pack.

Home cooks pick up our 100 g packs. Cafés, food trucks and caterers rely on our 1 kg packs to keep their menus consistent on busy days.
